ARCHCAPS: CCDI CAPSTONE E-LIBRARY

The ARCHCAPS: CCDI Capstone E-Library system is designed to address the long-standing problem of storing, organizing, and accessing capstone projects within the Computer Communication Development Institute (CCDI). For years, the institution has relied heavily on physical copies of research outputs, which are kept in shelves, folders, or filing cabinets. As more students complete their capstone projects each year, these storage areas become overcrowded and difficult to maintain.
